M O T I VAT I O N - D E A F P E R S O N S
• Deaf persons without possibility to easily express their own needs and decisions tend to suffer
depression.
• Deaf kids require a simple and quick way to express themselves while they learn some language (ex.
signal language, written or spoken languages). Specially when they are not able to express spoken
language.
• Deaf persons require assistance to communicate their needs and decision to people that haven’t
learned the signal language.
• Support the learning of other languages using a simple communication tool.
• It is possible for a deaf person to learn written language enabling him to read and write it. Allowing
him to develop knowledge and skills provided in schools and acquire a technical and professional
career.
P R O P O S A L
• A Tablet UI
• To navigate between pages within a category.
• To easily and quickly reach action buttons that:
• can speak complex and common phrases.
• can be easily configured.
• can be used via physical numeric keyboard.
• can be used to write and speak text phrases.
• allow to interact with another non connected tablet for dialogue purposes.

A D D I T I O N A L F E AT U R E S
• Each button may have icon/image instead of short text label.
• Its possible to add sound effects and haptic vibrations for the buttons.
• The display may also reproduce images or videos.
• Pages can be created configuring their buttons.
• Categories can be created and configured attaching pages into it.
• Buttons can be configured in label / icon /color /action /sound effect / haptic effect / display output.
• Special Pages for quick reply can be created for 1-1 interaction with another person.

• It may be possible to trigger some buttons using speech commands.
